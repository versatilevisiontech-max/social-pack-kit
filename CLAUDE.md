# CLAUDE.md — social pack

## Job
Fill one post pack from `templates/post-pack.md`. Save it as `drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-short-name.md`.

## Rules
- Voice: calm, precise, visual. No hype words. No exclamation marks.
- Say which Instagram account: A or B. If unknown, ask before writing that block.
- Mark studies as studies. Never invent a client, a price, or a press quote.
- No inventions or confidential technical work, even if the user pasted some by mistake. Leave it out and say so.
- Hashtags only if asked, max five.
- Do not tell the user the pack is posted. It is a draft until they approve.

## Length
- Instagram: 1–2 sentences
- X: 1–2 sentences
- LinkedIn and Facebook: one short paragraph
- YouTube: hook line, then 20–35 seconds of spoken words
